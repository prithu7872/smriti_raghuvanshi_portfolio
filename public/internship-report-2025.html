Literature Review

The role of social media in modern business communication and marketing.

THEME 1

Shift in role of social media

Social media has evolved from a secondary tool to a primary medium for brand engagement and communication.

KAPLAN AND HAENLEIN (2010)

Interactive communication

According to Kaplan and Haenlein (2010), social media facilitates a two-way dialogue, enabling real-time feedback between companies and their users.

THEME 3

Relationship building

The interactive nature of social media allows brands to build stronger connections with their audience and gain valuable consumer insights.

MANGOLD AND FAULDS (2009)

Hybrid promotional element

Mangold and Faulds (2009) state that social media is a hybrid promotional tool that combines traditional marketing techniques with word-of-mouth influence, making it more powerful than conventional channels.

Engagement ecosystem. Collectively, these studies suggest that social media is more than just a promotional tool; it is an ecosystem of engagement and influence that shapes modern consumer behavior.

The literature review demonstrates a foundational understanding of the strategic importance of social media in a data-driven marketing and sales context. It provides a theoretical basis for the project, connecting the practical problem statement with established academic research on digital marketing.

My Role

As a Sales Intern at Mozo Hunt Pvt. Ltd., my primary role was to drive revenue growth by executing a complete sales cycle.

Prospecting & Lead Generation

Created targeted client lists from Mozo Hunt's database and independent research, focusing on high-demand sectors like Education, Pharma, and IT.

Client Outreach

Approached prospects through WhatsApp, LinkedIn, phone calls, and emails, tailoring communication to client preferences.

Sales Pitching

Presented Mozo Hunt's unique selling points (verified data, sector targeting, affordability) using digital brochures and presentations.

Objection Handling & Negotiation

Addressed client concerns about pricing, data accuracy, and ROI; offered trial samples and value-added deals to build trust.

Closing & Follow-up

Closed deals through secure online payments, ensured timely delivery of services, collected feedback, and maintained long-term client engagement.

Conclusion

In conclusion, managing this ad campaign provided me with a hands-on learning experience in digital marketing. It allowed me to not only understand how to set up an ad but also how to interpret performance data and gain insights into audience behavior.

This practical knowledge—from understanding key metrics like link clicks and reach to analyzing geographic and demographic data—is crucial for creating and executing effective marketing strategies that produce measurable results and drive business growth.

Beyond the technical aspects, this experience taught me the importance of continuous optimization. By regularly monitoring the campaign's performance, I was able to make real-time adjustments that led to a higher return on investment and a deeper understanding of what resonates with a target audience.
