THE VISUAL IDEA

A familiar campus. A personal point of view.

The existing portfolio record describes this project as a celebration of the university through students' photography and authentic campus perspectives. The page treats that description as the creative premise—not as a claim about specific photographs or campaign results that were not supplied.

01

Notice the detail

Look beyond the obvious landmark. Small visual details can make a place feel personal and recognizable.

02

Let students frame it

Use a student-centred perspective as the organising idea, rather than presenting only an institutional viewpoint.

03

Turn frames into stories

Give each image a role in a larger visual narrative about campus life and the people who experience it.
