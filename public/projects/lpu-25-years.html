A NUMBER / A QUESTION / A STORY
COINCIDENCE?
THE CREATIVE DEVICE

Make the audience notice 25.

The supplied teaser uses repetition as its hook: the number is encountered in a notebook, a book page, a building plate and a jersey before the film asks whether the pattern is coincidence or a sign.

01

Seed

A student studies. The number first appears as a handwritten note.

02

Repeat

The number reappears in familiar campus and study contexts.

03

Escalate

Each appearance makes the pattern more noticeable.

04

Question

The closing text turns recognition into curiosity: coincidence or sign?

The retrospective side of the project title invites a look back at the university's journey. The source materials supplied for this page do not include archival photographs, dated milestones or a verified historical timeline.
