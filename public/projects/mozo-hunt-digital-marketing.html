ROLE & RESPONSIBILITIES

A complete sales cycle, step by step.

The report describes five connected responsibilities, from identifying suitable prospects to maintaining relationships after delivery.

01 / FIND

Prospecting & lead generation

Created targeted client lists from the Mozo Hunt database and independent research, focusing on Education, Pharma and IT.

02 / CONNECT

Client outreach

Approached prospects through WhatsApp, LinkedIn, phone calls and email, adapting communication to client preferences.

03 / PRESENT

Sales pitching

Presented verified data, sector targeting and affordability using digital brochures and presentations.

04 / RESOLVE

Objection handling

Addressed concerns about pricing, data accuracy and ROI; offered trial samples and value-added deals.

05 / CLOSE

Closing & follow-up

Closed deals through secure online payments, coordinated service delivery, collected feedback and maintained engagement.

Source distinction: the completion certificate describes the internship field as Marketing and HR; the report's role section identifies the position as Sales Intern. Both descriptions are retained in their respective source contexts.
