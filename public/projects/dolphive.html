The problem

The problem with traditional workforce management

Modern corporate organizations struggle with biased task allocation, lack of transparency, unequal workload distribution, subjective managerial decision-making, favoritism, reduced employee morale, productivity imbalance and leadership pipeline gaps. Traditional systems frequently depend on manual allocation and managerial discretion.

Traditional model
    Reading this

    These are the problem landscape identified by the project, not a claim that every traditional HR system suffers from all of them. Dolphive proposes competency-based allocation, transparent governance and data-informed workforce management.
